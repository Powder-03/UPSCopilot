# Hyperframes Composition Brief: UPSCopilot (Tactile Light Editorial Edition)

## Objective
Create a punchy, 22-second launch video for UPSCopilot inspired by high-end tactile print and editorial brutalism: warm ivory parchment canvas (`#FAF7F2`), deep carbon sumi ink (`#1C1917`), rich warm terracotta accents (`#C2410C`), archival crimson stamps (`#B91C1C`), crisp physical stationery card, and zero harsh neon AI colors.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape (1280x720)
- Duration: 22 seconds

## Creative Direction & Color System
- **Tone**: Bold, kinky, swaggering, editorial brutalist — completely free of harsh AI neons.
- **Palette**:
  - Studio Outer Canvas: `#EAE5DC` (Warm linen stone)
  - Canvas Paper: `#FAF7F2` (Warm archival parchment) with natural paper grain
  - Primary Typography: Deep Carbon Ink `#1C1917` (warm sumi ink)
  - Subhead Text: Soft Charcoal `#57534E`
  - Inverted Pill Badges: Deep Carbon Ink `#1C1917` with Warm Ivory text (`#FAF7F2`)
  - Accents:
    - Warm Terracotta / Clay: `#C2410C` (Pillars, highlights, queue time)
    - Archival Rubber Stamp Crimson: `#B91C1C` (Fraud detected, stamp slam)
    - Forest Olive: `#15803D` (Passed, diagram detection, verified status)
  - Physical Stationery Card: Crisp Warm White (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle warm hairline borders (`#E6E1D8`) and natural drop shadows

## Typography
- Display Sans: `Syne` (700/800 weight, tight negative tracking `-0.045em`)
- Editorial Display Serif: `Instrument Serif` (italic, 82px)
- Monospace: `JetBrains Mono`
