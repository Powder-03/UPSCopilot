# GS-3: Agriculture, Food Security & Farm Economics (Canonical Dossier)

## 1. The Minimum Support Price (MSP) & Swaminathan Commission

### National Commission on Farmers (M.S. Swaminathan Committee, 2004–2006)
- **Core Recommendation**:
  - The Commission recommended that the **Minimum Support Price (MSP) should be at least 50% more than the weighted average cost of production ($C_2 + 50\%$)**.
- **Three Methodologies of Cost of Production (CACP Calculations)**:
  1. **$A_2$ Cost**: Covers all paid-out expenses incurred directly by the farmer in cash and kind (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, hired human labor, tractor/bullock fuel, irrigation charges, leased-in land rent).
  2. **$A_2 + \text{FL}$ Cost**: Includes $A_2$ costs plus the **imputed value of unpaid Family Labor**.
     - Currently, the Central Government sets MSP at **$1.5 \text{ times } (A_2 + \text{FL})$** for all mandated crops.
  3. **$C_2$ Cost (Comprehensive Cost)**:
     - The gold standard advocated by farmer unions and the Swaminathan Committee.
     - Includes $A_2 + \text{FL}$ PLUS the **imputed rent of owned agricultural land** and **imputed interest on owned fixed capital assets/machinery**.
- **Crops Covered Under MSP (22 Mandated Crops + 1)**:
  - *7 Cereals*: Paddy, Wheat, Maize, Sorghum (Jowar), Pearl Millet (Bajra), Barley, Ragi.
  - *5 Pulses*: Gram (Chana), Tur (Arhar), Moong, Urad, Lentil (Masur).
  - *8 Oilseeds*: Groundnut, Rapeseed-Mustard, Soybean, Sunflower, Sesame, Safflower, Nigerseed.
  - *4 Commercial Crops*: Raw Cotton, Raw Jute, Copra, De-husked Coconut.
  - *Sugarcane*: Governed by **Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP)** under the Sugarcane (Control) Order 1966, linked to sugar recovery rate.
- **Structural Issues with Current MSP Regime**:
  - High geographical concentration: Procurement predominantly benefits farmers in Punjab, Haryana, Madhya Pradesh, and Western UP, leaving Eastern and Southern states exposed to distress sales.
  - Crop distortion: Favors water-intensive Paddy and Wheat over climate-resilient, nutritionally superior Millets (*Shree Anna*) and pulses.
  - Lack of statutory backing: MSP is an executive price support mechanism announced by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) on recommendations of the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices (CACP); it is not a legally enforceable right.

## 2. Statutory Food Security: NFSA 2013 Architecture

### National Food Security Act, 2013 (NFSA / Right to Food)
- Enacted to convert food security from a discretionary welfare scheme into a **legal entitlement**.
- **Population Coverage (Section 3)**:
  - Legally covers **up to 75% of the rural population** and **up to 50% of the urban population** (overall ~67% of India's total population, over 81 crore citizens).
- **Two Beneficiary Categories**:
  1. **Priority Households (PHH)**: Entitled to **5 kg of foodgrains per person per month**.
  2. **Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY - Poorest of the Poor)**: Entitled to **35 kg of foodgrains per household per month**.
- **Statutory Prices & PMGKAY**:
  - Statutory rates originally fixed at ₹3/kg for rice, ₹2/kg for wheat, and ₹1/kg for coarse grains.
  - Under **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY)**, foodgrains are currently provided **100% free of cost** across all PHH and AAY beneficiaries.
- **Life-Cycle Nutritional Support**:
  - *Pregnant women & lactating mothers*: Free nutritious meal during pregnancy and 6 months after birth through Anganwadi centers, plus maternity benefit of not less than **₹6,000** (under Pradhan Mantri Matru Vandana Yojana - PMMVY).
  - *Children (6 months to 14 years)*: Free hot-cooked meals meeting nutritional standards under PM POSHAN (Mid-Day Meal Scheme) and ICDS.
- **Food Security Allowance (Section 8)**:
  - If the public distribution system fails to supply entitled foodgrains within prescribed time, beneficiaries have a statutory right to receive a cash Food Security Allowance from the State Government.

## 3. Agricultural Market Reforms & Technology Integration

### Electronic National Agriculture Market (e-NAM)
- A pan-India electronic trading portal launched in 2016 to network existing Agricultural Produce Market Committee (APMC) physical mandis into a **unified national market for agricultural commodities**.
- Promotes transparent price discovery based on actual quality assaying, electronic bidding, online payments, and direct transfer into farmer bank accounts, eliminating multiple layers of commission agents (*arthiyas*).

### Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- Comprehensive crop insurance scheme replacing NAIS and Modified NAIS.
- **Standardized Actuarial Premiums Paid by Farmers**:
  - **Kharif Food & Oilseed Crops**: Maximum **2.0%** of sum insured.
  - **Rabi Food & Oilseed Crops**: Maximum **1.5%** of sum insured.
  - **Annual Commercial & Horticultural Crops**: Maximum **5.0%** of sum insured.
  - Difference between actuarial premium and farmer contribution is shared 50:50 between the Centre and States (90:10 for North-Eastern states).
- **Coverage of Risks**: Covers pre-sowing prevention, mid-season localized adversities (hailstorms, landslides, cloudbursts), and post-harvest losses up to 14 days after harvesting. Use of satellite imagery, remote sensing, and drones for rapid crop damage assessment.

### Direct Income Support: PM-KISAN
- **Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi (PM-KISAN)**:
  - Central Sector Scheme providing an assured income support of **₹6,000 per year** to all landholding farmer families across India.
  - Disbursed in three equal four-monthly installments of **₹2,000 each** directly into Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts via DBT.

## 4. Irrigation, Resource Use & Subsidy Rationalization

### Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY)
- Merged Accelerated Irrigation Benefit Programme (AIBP), Integrated Watershed Management Programme (IWMP), and On-Farm Water Management.
- Motto: **"Har Khet Ko Pani"** (extending physical access of water) and **"Per Drop More Crop"** (enhancing water-use efficiency through micro-irrigation: drip and sprinkler systems).

### Fertilizer Subsidies & The Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS)
- **The Urea Disparity**:
  - Urea is under statutory price control (Maximum Retail Price set by Government; difference between cost of production and MRP paid as subsidy).
  - This caused massive over-application of nitrogen, skewing the ideal N:P:K ratio (ideal 4:2:1) to over 8:3:1 in states like Punjab, leading to soil acidification, groundwater nitrate toxicity, and micro-nutrient depletion.
  - Mitigation: **100% Neem-Coated Urea** mandated to slow nitrogen release and prevent illegal diversion to chemical industries; introduction of **Nano Urea (Liquid)**.
- **Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS, Introduced 2010)**:
  - Applied to Non-Urea fertilizers (Phosphatic and Potassic - P&K fertilizers).
  - Fixed per-kg subsidy announced annually on primary nutrients: Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), Potassium (K), and Sulphur (S).
