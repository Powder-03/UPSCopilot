# GS-1: Indian Society, Demography & Social Justice (Canonical Dossier)

## 1. Women's Empowerment & The Gender Landscape

### Female Labor Force Participation Rate (FLFPR) & Unpaid Care Economy
- **Statistical Trajectory (PLFS Trends)**:
  - India's female labor force participation historically exhibited a stubborn dip despite rising female educational attainment, fitting the classic **"U-shaped hypothesis"** (as household incomes rise with initial development, women withdraw from distress manual labor; they re-enter only when high-productivity formal service/knowledge jobs become accessible).
  - Recent Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS) reports indicate a recovery in female LFPR (rising above 37% by usual status), largely driven by self-employment in rural agriculture and micro-enterprises.
- **The "Unpaid Care Work" Burden**:
  - The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) **Time Use Survey (TUS)** revealed that Indian women spend an average of **299 minutes per day** on unpaid domestic and caregiving services, compared to just **97 minutes per day** spent by men.
  - This structural "motherhood penalty" and double burden restricts women's physical mobility, upskilling, and participation in formal paid employment.
- **Institutional & Statutory Landmark Protections**:
  - **Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023)**:
    - Inserts **Article 330A** (Lok Sabha reservation), **Article 332A** (State Legislative Assemblies reservation), and **Article 239AA(2)(b)** (Delhi Assembly).
    - Reserves **one-third (33%) of all seats for women** for a period of 15 years, subject to delimitation conducted after the first census published following the Act.
  - **Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005 (PWDVA)**:
    - Civil law recognizing emotional, physical, sexual, and economic abuse. Guarantees the woman's right to reside in a shared household (*Satish Chander Ahuja v. Sneha Ahuja*, 2020) and mandates Protection Officers.
  - **Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013 (POSH Act)**:
    - Codified the historic **Vishaka Guidelines** (*Vishaka v. State of Rajasthan*, 1997).
    - Mandates every workplace with $\ge 10$ employees to constitute an **Internal Complaints Committee (ICC)** chaired by a senior female employee.
  - **MTP Amendment Act, 2021 & Reproductive Autonomy**:
    - Expanded upper gestation limit for medical termination of pregnancy from 20 to 24 weeks for vulnerable categories (rape survivors, minors, anomalies).
    - Landmark Supreme Court Ruling in *X v. Principal Secretary, Health and Family Welfare Department (2022)*: Affirmed that reproductive autonomy, bodily integrity, and right to abort belong equally to **unmarried women**, recognizing marital rape within the scope of MTP Act.

## 2. Caste Dynamics, Affirmative Action & Social Mobilization

### Evolution of Caste Politics
- **From Ritual Hierarchy to Political Power Bloc**:
  - Caste has evolved from an ascriptive ritual system (purity vs pollution) into a powerful modern vehicle for political mobilization, democratic assertiveness, and bargaining for public resources.
  - Rise of regional caste-based parties and intermediate agricultural castes (OBC assertion since the 1990s).
- **Sub-Categorization of Other Backward Classes (OBCs)**:
  - **Justice G. Rohini Commission (Article 340)**:
    - Constituted to examine the inequitable distribution of benefits among the ~2,600 castes listed in the Central OBC list.
    - Findings demonstrated that a tiny minority (less than 10% of OBC communities) cornered over 50% of reserved public jobs and central higher education seats, while nearly 1,000 sub-castes had virtually zero representation.
- **Economically Weaker Sections (EWS Quota)**:
  - **103rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 2019**:
    - Inserted **Article 15(6)** and **Article 16(6)** permitting the State to provide up to **10% reservation** in educational admissions and public employment for citizens belonging to Economically Weaker Sections other than SC, ST, and OBC.
  - **Janhit Abhiyan v. Union of India (2022 - 5-Judge Constitution Bench)**:
    - Upheld the constitutional validity of the 103rd Amendment by a 3:2 majority.
    - Held that economic criteria alone can form a valid basis for classification under the Constitution, and that the 50% ceiling rule laid down in *Indra Sawhney (1992)* applies strictly to social and educational reservations under Articles 15(4) and 16(4), and is not an inflexible constitutional barrier.

## 3. Aging Society, Population Dynamics & Internal Migration

### The "Silver Economy" & Elderly Welfare
- **UNFPA India Ageing Report (2023)**:
  - India is transitioning towards an aging society: The elderly population (60+ years) is projected to double from 10.5% (149 million in 2022) to **20.8% (347 million by 2050)**.
  - By 2046, the number of elderly citizens in India will surpass the total number of children (0–14 years).
  - *Feminization of Aging*: Women have higher life expectancy than men, leading to high rates of widowhood, lack of asset ownership, and economic dependency among senior women.
- **Statutory Safeguards**:
  - **Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007**: Creates statutory Maintenance Tribunals; makes it a legal duty for children and heirs to provide monthly maintenance; enables revocation of property transfers made by elderly parents if children neglect their basic needs.
  - Seniorcare Aging Growth Engine (SAGE) initiative promoting start-ups in elderly healthcare, assisted living, and digital literacy.

### Internal Migration Patterns & Social Inclusion
- **Magnitude & Drivers**:
  - Over 100 million internal migrant workers in India (Census 2011 and Economic Survey 2017).
  - Dominated by short-term, circular labor migration from high-outmigration corridors (Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, Odisha, Jharkhand) to high-growth economic hubs (Maharashtra, Delhi-NCR, Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu).
- **Vulnerabilities & Institutional Reforms**:
  - Informality, lack of domicile proof, denial of local voting rights, exclusion from local subsidized public distribution systems (PDS).
  - **One Nation One Ration Card (ONORC)**: Enables biometric-authenticated portability of subsidized foodgrain under the National Food Security Act (NFSA 2013) across any Fair Price Shop in the country.
  - **e-Shram Portal**: National database of unorganized workers linking migrant workers, gig workers, and domestic help to social security schemes via Universal Account Numbers (UAN).

## 4. Urbanization Dynamics & Sustainable Slum Redevelopment

### Nature of Indian Urban Growth
- **Scale**: Urban population stands at 31.16% (Census 2011) and is projected to exceed 40% (over 600 million people) by 2035.
- **Census Towns Phenomenon**: Settlements with population $\ge 5,000$, $\ge 75\%$ male non-agricultural working population, and density $\ge 400\text{ persons/km}^2$, but **lacking a formal statutory urban local body (Municipality)**. They remain governed by rural Panchayats, causing acute civic infrastructure deficits.
- **Urban Sprawl & Slum Proliferation**:
  - Inability of formal housing markets to match rapid rural-urban influx leads to extensive informal settlements and slums lacking basic piped water, sanitation, and title tenure (e.g., Dharavi in Mumbai).
  - Policy shift from slum clearance to **In-Situ Slum Rehabilitation (ISSR)** under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Urban (PMAY-U) and public-private partnership models.
